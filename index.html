🏋️ IronLog

Gym & Hypertrophy Tracker

IronLog is a free Progressive Web App (PWA) for tracking gym workouts, monitoring hypertrophy progress, and following structured training routines like Push-Pull-Legs, Upper/Lower, and Full Body splits. Log sets, reps, and weights — with offline support and optional cloud sync across devices.

Features include an 82-exercise library across 11 muscle groups, 15 pre-built workout routines, muscle recovery tracking, golden-era proportion targets, and smart progressive overload suggestions. No subscription required. Sign in with Google or email to sync across devices.
