Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

IronLog ("we", "our", or "the app") is a personal gym and hypertrophy tracking Progressive Web App (PWA). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

When you use IronLog, we may collect:

  • Email address — if you create an account using email/password sign-up
  • Google account info — name and email, if you sign in with Google OAuth
  • Workout data — exercises, sets, reps, weights, and check-in history you log in the app

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate you and maintain your session
  • To sync your workout history across devices (cloud backup)
  • To display your progress within the app

We do not sell, share, or use your data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

  • Locally: Workout data is stored in your browser's localStorage for offline access
  • Cloud: If you sign in, data is synced to Supabase (our backend provider) and stored securely with Row Level Security — only you can access your data
  • Authentication: Handled by Supabase Auth and Google OAuth 2.0

4. Google Sign-In

If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your Google account's name and email address via OAuth 2.0. We do not access your Google Drive, Contacts, or any other Google services. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

5. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You may delete your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

6. Cookies & Tracking

IronLog does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. Supabase uses session tokens stored in localStorage for authentication purposes only.

7. Children's Privacy

IronLog is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
