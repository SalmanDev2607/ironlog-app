Privacy Policy

IronLog – Gym & Hypertrophy Tracker

Last updated: 25 October 2026  |  Effective: 25 October 2026

IronLog is a free, open-source Progressive Web App (PWA) for personal gym workout tracking. We are committed to your privacy. We do not sell your data, run ads, or share your information with third parties for commercial purposes.

1. Who We Are

IronLog ("the App", "we", "us") is a personal fitness tracking application available at https://salmandev2607.github.io/ironlog-app. The app is developed and maintained independently by the IronLog project. For privacy questions, contact us at abdulsalman451@gmail.com.

2. What Data We Collect

IronLog operates in two modes:

2a. Guest Mode (no account required)

When you use IronLog without signing in:

  • No personal data is collected or transmitted to any server
  • Workout data (exercises, sets, reps, weights, check-ins) is stored only in your browser's localStorage on your device
  • Data never leaves your device in guest mode
  • Clearing browser data will erase all locally stored workout history

2b. Signed-In Mode (account required for cloud sync)

If you choose to create an account or sign in with Google, we collect and store:

Data TypePurposeStored Where
Email addressAccount identification & loginSupabase Auth (encrypted)
Display name (Google only)Personalise your experienceSupabase Auth
Workout historyCross-device cloud syncSupabase Database (encrypted at rest)
Auth session tokenKeep you logged inBrowser localStorage

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • NO Location or GPS data
  • NO Health metrics (heart rate, steps, etc.)
  • NO Payment or financial information
  • NO Contacts, calendar, or other device data
  • NO Tracking cookies or advertising identifiers
  • NO Usage analytics or crash reporting services
  • NO Any data beyond what you explicitly enter into the app

4. How We Use Your Data

We use the information collected solely to:

  1. Authenticate your account — verify your identity when you sign in
  2. Sync your workout data — back up and restore your training history across devices
  3. Display your progress — show workout history, personal records, and muscle recovery within the app

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, marketing emails, or any purpose beyond the core app functionality described above.

5. Google Sign-In (OAuth 2.0)

IronLog offers "Sign in with Google" as a convenient login option. When you use this:

  • You are redirected to Google's secure authentication page
  • Google shares only your email address and display name with IronLog
  • IronLog does not access your Google Drive, Gmail, Contacts, Calendar, or any other Google service
  • The specific OAuth scopes requested are: openid, email, profile — the minimum required for identity only
  • You can revoke IronLog's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions

6. Data Storage & Security

Local Storage

Workout data is stored in your browser's localStorage. This data is private to your browser and device. It is not shared unless you are signed in with cloud sync enabled.

Cloud Storage (Supabase)

Account and workout data for signed-in users is stored on Supabase, our backend-as-a-service provider. Supabase stores data in encrypted form and uses Row Level Security (RLS) — a database-level access control that ensures you can only ever read and write your own data. No other user or administrator can query your rows.

Supabase's full privacy policy: supabase.com/privacy

Encryption

  • All data transmitted between your device and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Data is encrypted at rest in Supabase's infrastructure
  • Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are hashed using Supabase Auth's secure methods

8. Data Retention

  • Local data: Retained in your browser until you clear site data or uninstall the app
  • Cloud data: Retained while your account is active
  • Account deletion: You may request deletion of your account and all associated cloud data by emailing abdulsalman451@gmail.com. We will process your request within 30 days.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and data
  • Portability: Request your workout data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection: Object to or restrict certain processing activities

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at abdulsalman451@gmail.com.

10. Cookies & Tracking

IronLog does not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics (such as Google Analytics). The only storage we use is:

  • localStorage — for storing workout data and auth session tokens locally on your device
  • sessionStorage — for temporary session flags (cleared when you close the browser tab)

11. Children's Privacy

IronLog is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EU/UK). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

12. International Users

IronLog is available worldwide. By using the app and creating an account, you consent to your data being processed and stored in the regions where Supabase operates its infrastructure (primarily USA and EU). Supabase complies with applicable data protection laws.

13. Service Workers & Offline Use

IronLog uses a Service Worker to enable offline functionality and PWA installation. The Service Worker caches app files (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) on your device to allow the app to work without an internet connection. It does not cache or transmit personal data.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
